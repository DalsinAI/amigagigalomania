Gigalomania: licences
=====================

The game: Gigalomania 1.0.1 by Mark Harman and contributors (a remake of
Mega-Lo-Mania; it holds nothing of the original game),
https://sourceforge.net/projects/gigalomania/, git commit
3bb9e19c7ab515d853fb5057be30e73dd27839d8.

- The program is under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or (at
  your option) any later version (COPYING.txt; upstream's own copy,
  gpl.txt, is left out of its git tree, so the port carries the text in
  amiga/COPYING-GPL2.txt). TinyXML (TinyXML/) is under the zlib licence,
  as its source files say.
- The graphics (gfx/), sounds (sound/), music (music/) and islands
  (islands/) are upstream's own, unchanged, under the licences that
  readme.html lists file by file in its "Licences" section: the GPL v2 or
  later (Mark Harman's own work, and pieces from FreeCiv and Widelands),
  CC BY 3.0, CC BY-SA 3.0 (or later, or GPL v3 or later), CC0 and GPL v2.
  All of them allow the files to be copied and passed on, the CC BY and
  CC BY-SA ones with the credit readme.html gives, which is why
  readme.html goes into the drawer with them. Debian distributes the same
  files in main (debian/copyright lists each one with its licence).
  Credits include: Rob Hunter (speech, CC0), Sindre Froyn (music, CC BY
  3.0), _ghost ("Warm Ink", CC BY 3.0), Macrofeet, Mystic Mike, wulax, yd,
  mcco0055, Clint Bellanger, Lamoot, rubberduck, StumpyStrust and the
  others readme.html names.
- The icons (amiga/icons/) are drawn from upstream's gigalomania64.png,
  under the game's GPL.
- The Amiga port's own files (amiga/) are GPL 2 or later, like the game.
  amiga/amiga_screens.c comes from the AmiCradle Team's Seven Kingdoms and
  Abbaye ports.

At run time the program uses GFOpenGPU's SDL2.module, SDL2_image.module
and SDL2_mixer.module (SDL 2, SDL_image and SDL_mixer: zlib licence),
which are installed with GFOpenGPU and are not part of this drawer.
